# Required Images List

This document outlines all the images needed for the project, along with a brief description of what each shot should show.


## How to supply files


## Shoot these first

✅ - **home-hero**: The first thing every visitor from Instagram sees.
✅ - **product-modak-candle**: The hero product and the brand's whole wedge.
✅ - **product-modak-candle-lit**: Proves the mithai is genuinely a candle.
✅ - **product-laddu-candle**: Second signature piece; the Diwali seller.
✅ - **bulk-hero**: Opens the highest-value page on the site.

## Required — pages


### Home — Hero

✅ - **home-hero**: Full-bleed hero. Modak candles on a brass platter with fresh marigolds, warm natural side light, shallow depth of field. Leave clear negative space in the left third for the headline.

✅ - **bulk-banner**: Wide shot of a completed bulk order — rows of boxed candles with tags. Warm light, expose slightly under so white text sits comfortably on top.
